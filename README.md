# 🧠 Recall

### Study once. Recall better.

Students often spend hours studying, but later struggle to remember what they learned.

**Recall** is a student-focused revision web app that helps solve the  
**“I studied it, but I forgot it”** problem through flashcards, spaced revision, self-testing, and progress tracking.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Students often:

- Forget topics after studying them
- Don't know when to revise again
- Spend more time rereading than testing themselves
- Find it difficult to track revision progress
- Struggle to maintain consistent study habits

## 💡 Solution

Recall provides a simple revision system that allows students to:

- Create and review flashcards
- Schedule cards for future revision
- Rate cards as **Hard, Okay, or Easy**
- Test their knowledge with self-tests
- Track subject-wise progress
- Maintain a study streak
- Monitor their overall mastery

---

## ✨ Features

- 🗂️ **Flashcards** – Create, edit, search, and review study cards
- 🔄 **Spaced Revision** – Review cards based on their difficulty
- 🧪 **Self-Test** – Test yourself instead of simply rereading
- 📊 **Progress Tracking** – Monitor cards, mastery, and revision activity
- 📚 **Subjects** – Organize flashcards by subject
- 🔥 **Study Streak** – Keep track of consistent revision
- 🌙 **Dark Mode** – Switch between light and dark themes
- 💾 **Local Storage** – Save cards and progress directly in the browser
- 📱 **Responsive Design** – Works across different screen sizes
